About Nitesh

Bridging Precision Engineering with Cultural Heritage

A unique fusion of modern manufacturing, CAD design, and dedication to traditional Indian performing arts.

Hands-on Engineer & Cultural Ambassador

I am a Mechanical Engineering diploma student at Government Polytechnic, Mundagod (affiliated with the Department of Collegiate and Technical Education, Bangalore, Karnataka), passionate about precision CNC machining, 3D CAD modeling, manufacturing technology, industrial pumps, and electric motors.

I completed an intensive 16-week industrial internship in Advance Manufacturing Technology at the prestigious Govt. Tool Room and Training Centre (GTTC Hubli - Indo-Danish Project), mastering 3D CAD modeling, 2D drafting with GD&T, rapid prototyping, and precision 3D printing. Furthermore, I achieved top Grade "A+" (>80%) in CNC Programming & Operation (Turning & Milling) from VTU-NASD Dandeli (160 Hours).

Alongside engineering, I am a trained folk dancer and Indian bamboo flute player (Bansuri) with deep reverence for Indian cultural heritage. Having performed at the world-famous Mysore Dasara Jamboo Savari and on the Hon'ble Chief Minister’s Stage on multiple occasions, I bring discipline, stage presence, and stamina into every endeavor.

I am also actively involved as a dedicated volunteer (Karyakarta) with the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), contributing to community development, social initiatives, and cultural preservation across Karnataka.

🎭

Yakshagana

Coastal & Malenadu Karnataka

Majestic traditional theatre and dance combining elaborate vibrant costumes, heavy headgear (Mudi), intricate footwork, expressive Navarasa acting, and mythological storytelling.

  • Classical footwork & rhythm
  • Mythological character portrayal
  • Dynamic stage presence & stamina
🥁

Kamsale

Southern Karnataka Folk Art

A high-energy, rhythmic folk dance dedicated to Lord Male Mahadeshwara using heavy brass cymbals (Kamsale), requiring lightning-fast reflexes and acrobatic synchronization.

  • Synchronized brass cymbal beats
  • Acrobatic leaps and spins
  • Spiritual folklore narration
🥢

Kolata & Tamate

Traditional Folk Dances

Mastery of Kolata (traditional rhythmic stick dance with interlocking group patterns) and Tamate (high-energy percussion dance that electrifies cultural processions).

  • Complex group choreography
  • High-tempo percussion rhythm
  • Mass cultural parade performances
